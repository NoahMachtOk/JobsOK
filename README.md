# JobsOK

JobsOK is a lightweight and reliable Jobsystem made with Skript.

Jobs:
» Miner
» Fighter
» Digger
» Timberjack (Lumberjack)
» Tourist
» Fisher
» Farmer

## Why JobsOK?

Because its made with Skript, it can be fully customized at any time or be integrated in other Skripts. 
JobsOK also has a lot of optimations, so it doesn't affect your server's TPS or bring lag.
